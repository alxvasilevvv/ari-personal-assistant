What a voice reminder app should actually do

Understand natural speech

“Remind me to submit the report Friday at 4” or “wake me at 7” — no rigid commands, no syntax to memorize. Works in English and Russian.

Put it where iOS expects

Ari writes entries into Apple Reminders and events into Calendar, so your other apps stay in sync. Alarms and timers ring like the system ones, with a countdown in the Dynamic Island.

Deliver in a format you can't miss

This is where Ari is different: at the scheduled time you get a phone call. You answer, she explains what she's calling about — medication, a meeting, an errand on the way home.

Get started in three steps

  1. Install Ari — free, no credit card.
  2. Tap “Call Ari” (app, widget, Lock Screen, Control Center, Action button, or say “Call Ari” to Siri) and speak your reminder.
  3. At the set time, pick up the call. Anything irreversible (a message, a call to someone else) is confirmed by you first.

Hands-free use cases

  • Driving: “call me 20 minutes before I need to exit the highway.”
  • Shift work: “remind me to switch the boiler at 6 pm” — a ringing phone beats a missed banner.
  • Family: “call grandma at noon to check on her meds.”
  • Morning: a briefing with today's calendar — and, with Gmail connected, the emails that matter.
