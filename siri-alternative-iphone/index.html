Ari — Personal Assistant
Siri alternative · iPhone

A voice assistant for iPhone that finishes the job — and calls you back

Siri is fine at recording a task. The gap is everything after: follow-through, delivery, context. Ari is a free iPhone voice assistant that closes those gaps — while staying compatible with the Siri workflow you already have.

Download Ari on the App Store →

What you get beyond stock Siri

Follow-up as a phone call

Ask for a reminder and at the right time Ari actually dials you — an ordinary incoming call you can answer hands-free, not another silent banner in a stack of thirty.

She acts, doesn't just note

“Call mom” → dialed, one confirmation tap. “Text Ivan on WhatsApp” → drafted, ready to send. “Route home avoiding traffic” → opens in Maps. Alarms and timers ring like system ones with a Dynamic Island countdown.

Reads your day back to you

Connect Google Calendar and Gmail and Ari narrates your schedule and finds specific emails — the “morning briefing” Siri never quite delivers.

Remembers and writes first

Ari stores what you tell her and initiates contact when the moment arrives — in the app or via Telegram.

Not anti-Siri

Ari works alongside iOS, not against it: Reminders and Calendar entries she creates appear in Apple's own apps, and you can summon her through Siri itself (“Call Ari”), widgets, the Lock Screen, Control Center or the Action button. During a call, her “eyes” appear in the Dynamic Island.

Privacy model

The microphone is on only while you're calling. Contacts and calendar access is requested only when a task needs it. Irreversible actions always require your confirmation. Every user gets a private Ari — nobody reads your chats or memory — and account plus data can be deleted from Settings. Privacy policy →
