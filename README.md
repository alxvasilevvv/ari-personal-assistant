# Ari — Personal Assistant for iPhone

**Live page:** https://alxvasilevvv.github.io/ari-personal-assistant/ · **App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ari-personal-assistant/id6831932629

Ari is a personal assistant with a voice. Call her with one tap, from Siri or the Lock Screen, and just say what needs doing:

- **She calls you** — "call me at 9 about the meeting" → at 9 Ari calls like a regular incoming call and tells you why
- **Gets things done** — alarms and timers with Dynamic Island countdowns, entries in Reminders, events in Calendar, "call mom" / "text Ivan on WhatsApp" prepared for one-tap, Maps routes, Shortcuts, weather
- **Remembers and reaches out** — writes first when it's time, in the app or in Telegram
- **Mail & calendar** — connect Google Calendar and Gmail
- **Yours only** — give Ari a name and pick a voice; nobody else sees your conversations or memory
- **Always at hand** — Dynamic Island "eyes", widgets, Control Center / Action button "Call Ari", Siri

Free · iPhone · English & Russian · Made by CSA Project – FZCO · [Privacy](https://arahub.io/privacy)
