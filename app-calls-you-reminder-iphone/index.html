Ari — Personal Assistant
Guide · Call‑back reminders

Is there an app that actually calls you as a reminder?

Yes — and it's free. Ari is an iPhone personal assistant that takes things off your chest and puts them in your pocket: say “call me at 9” once, and at 9 she rings like a normal incoming call and tells you why.

Download Ari on the App Store →
“Call me at 10 about the dentist” — said once, inside a car, at 8:12 pm. At 10:00 the next morning Ari calls. No app to open, no notification to miss.

Why a call, not a notification?

A notification banner can be muted, buried under thirty others, or swiped away without reading. A phone call is impossible to ignore silently — and if you're driving, cooking or walking, it's the only reminder format that works without touching the phone. That's the core idea Ari is built around.

Common cases

  • Medication: “call me at 8 pm to take my pills” — a call you answer is far more reliable than a banner.
  • Elderly parents: no icons to tap; a call is familiar, and Ari asks for confirmation before anything irreversible.
  • Driving and commute: hands‑free reminders via Siri or the “Call Ari” Action‑button shortcut.
  • Shift workers and travellers: “call me at 5:40”, “call me two hours before my flight”.

What else she does on the phone

While you're talking: alarms and timers that ring like system ones (Dynamic Island countdown included), entries in Reminders, events in Calendar, “text Ivan on WhatsApp” prepared for one tap, routes in Maps, weather, your Shortcuts — and with Google Calendar + Gmail connected, she reads out your day and finds the email you asked about. She remembers what you tell her and writes first when the time comes — in the app or on Telegram.
