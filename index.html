How Ari works

📞 SHE CALLS YOU

Say “call me at 9 about the meeting” — at 9, Ari calls you like a regular incoming call and tells you why. Morning briefings, reminders, important emails — delivered on your schedule.

Gets things done on iPhone

Alarms and timers that ring like the system ones, with a countdown in the Dynamic Island. Entries land in Reminders, events in Calendar. “Call mom”, “text Ivan on WhatsApp” — Ari finds the contact and prepares the call or message; you just tap. Maps routes, your Shortcuts, weather.

Remembers and reaches out

Ari remembers what you tell her and writes first when it's time — in the app or in Telegram.

Mail and calendar

Connect Google Calendar and Gmail — Ari tells you what's on today and finds the email you need.

Always at hand

Ari's eyes in the Dynamic Island during a call. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. A “Call Ari” control for Control Center and the Action button. Siri: “Call Ari”, “Ask Ari…”, “What's next”.

Yours only

Give Ari a name and pick a voice. Everyone gets their own Ari: nobody else sees your conversations or memory.

You're in control

Irreversible actions only with your confirmation. The microphone works only during a call; Ari asks for contacts and calendar access when needed. Delete your account and all data right in Settings.

FAQ

Is Ari really free?

Ari is free to download on the App Store — no credit card required to start.

Which languages does she speak?

English and Russian.

How is my data handled?

The microphone works only during a call. Ari requests contacts and calendar access the moment it's needed. You can delete your account and all data directly in Settings. Privacy policy →

Do I need to install a new app?

Yes — Ari is an iPhone app that works alongside Siri, Reminders, Calendar, WhatsApp and Maps. Just download it and tap “Call Ari”.
